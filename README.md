Batesio provides free document capture worksheet tools for small accounting, real estate and professional firms, allowing users to capture documents from their own boxes, pages and lookups. The platform offers a paid plan that includes a client document portal, page numbering, retention records, and secure sharing features. Founded in 2026, Batesio aims to simplify document management for small businesses with a SaaS solution.

**Areas:** Document Management · Accounting · SaaS · Small Business · File Sharing
